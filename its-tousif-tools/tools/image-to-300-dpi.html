What is DPI? Why 300 DPI?

DPI (Dots Per Inch) defines print density. 300 DPI is the industry standard for sharp prints, magazines and photo books. Our tool sets the pHYs chunk in PNG and JFIF density in JPG, plus resizes pixels proportionally for true print size.

Will quality be lost?+
No, we use high-quality bicubic scaling via canvas and preserve original aspect ratio.
Is this real 300 DPI?+
Yes, we inject correct DPI headers: PNG pHYs and JPEG JFIF APP0 density fields, plus pixel adjustment.
